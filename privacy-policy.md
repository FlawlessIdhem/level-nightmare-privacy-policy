# Level Nightmare – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Level Nightmare handles information when you use the game.

Level Nightmare is a mobile platform game developed by an independent developer. You do not need to create an account to play.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device, including:

- Level progress and completed levels.
- Hearts and gameplay progress.
- Selected and unlocked characters.
- Language preferences.
- Music settings.
- Records used to manage when interstitial advertisements are shown.

This information is stored locally using your device's application storage. The game does not send this gameplay or settings information to its own servers.

You can remove locally stored game information by uninstalling the application or clearing its data through your device settings.

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Level Nightmare uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The game includes:
- Rewarded advertisements, which players choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards.
- Interstitial advertisements, which may appear after certain level completions.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information to provide and improve advertising services. Depending on the SDK and device settings, this may include:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Device identifiers.
- Advertising information, such as ads viewed.
- App interactions, such as ad views and interactions.
- Diagnostic and performance information.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please see:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected directly by the game.

Information may be processed by Google through its advertising services as described above. The game does not attach locally stored gameplay progress or character preferences to its own advertising requests.

## 5. Data Retention

Gameplay progress and preferences remain stored locally until they are removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its application data.

Information processed by Google through AdMob is subject to Google's own data retention practices and policies.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The game does not require players to provide personal information through account registration or user-submitted forms. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

## 7. Security

We aim to minimize the information handled by the game. Gameplay and preference information is stored within the application's local storage. Advertising services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's security practices.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised update date.
